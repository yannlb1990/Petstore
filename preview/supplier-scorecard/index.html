How the rankings may change

At present, strong advertised product and factory ratings are only clues. We have no laboratory report, sample inspection, delivery performance or product-specific claim history. A supplier that performs poorly during the first ten Australian deliveries should lose fulfilment and reliability points. A second manufacturer offering a higher unit price but lower defect and refund costs can be the better commercial choice.

Ask every factory to quote the exact same incoterm, shipping destination and packaging assumptions. Compare a ten-unit test order separately from a fifty-unit and a two-hundred-unit order. If a factory requires custom printing at a much larger MOQ, start with unbranded protective packaging and add a local printed insert where legally appropriate.
