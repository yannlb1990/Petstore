Affiliate operating rules

  1. Apply as PawTrail to each programme, using the public editorial website and honest description of its audience.
  2. Do not turn ordinary links into claimed commission links until an approved publisher tracking URL is provided.
  3. Use rel="sponsored nofollow" on approved affiliate tracking links, and disclose commission next to the recommendation.
  4. Ask permission before copying retailer product photographs or brand artwork into PawTrail. Use authorised data feeds or original images.
  5. Record clicks by recommendation, outbound conversion and approved commission separately from PawTrail's own store orders.
  6. Keep medical, safety and feeding recommendations evidence-based; apply partner review or expert review where appropriate.

Do not use paid advertisement direct-to-merchant links unless a particular programme explicitly permits it. The strongest role for these recommendations is as editorial cross-sells underneath relevant PawTrail products and in the guides.
